Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Applies to the NeuralFlow Prompt Toolkit website.

This Privacy Policy explains how HUSAIN AMEER, operating from Egypt (“we”, “us”), handles data through this website. Contact: hessan.amer91@gmail.com.

1. What this product is

NeuralFlow Prompt Toolkit is a free browser tool that helps you write structured prompts for AI assistants. There are no accounts and no server-side storage of your prompts.

2. Data stored on your device (localStorage)

When you click “Save current prompt” in the toolkit, your prompt is stored in your own browser's localStorage under the key neuralflow_prompts_v1. This data:

  • stays only on your device and is never sent to us;
  • can be deleted anytime via the Delete button in the app or by clearing your browser's site data;
  • is lost if you clear browser data, switch browsers, or use private/incognito mode without persistence.

3. Data we do not collect

  • We do not require accounts, names, or signups to use the free tool.
  • The toolkit makes no external API calls with your prompt text — it only assembles text locally in the page.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

4. Contact emails

If you email us at hessan.amer91@gmail.com (for example for support or a refund request), we receive your email address and message content and use them only to respond. We keep such correspondence as long as needed to handle your request.

5. Payments

The optional Pro Prompt Pack is sold as a digital download. Payments are processed by Creem, acting as Merchant of Record. Creem processes your payment details under its own privacy policy at checkout. We do not see or store your card details.

6. Analytics and tracking

We do not currently use analytics, advertising cookies, or cross-site trackers on this site. If that changes, this policy will be updated before any tracker is added.

7. Your rights

Because prompts are stored only in your browser, you control them directly. For any other data (such as emails you sent us), you may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing hessan.amer91@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the “Last updated” date above.
