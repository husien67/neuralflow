FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Is the tool really free?

Yes. The prompt builder, the live preview, copy button, 12 starter templates and browser saving are all free with no account and no usage limits. The only paid item is the optional $9 Pro Prompt Pack file.

Do I need an account? Where are my prompts stored?

No account. Prompts you save are stored only in your own browser's localStorage on your device. We never see them. Clearing your browser data will delete them — see our Privacy Policy.

Does this tool call an AI or send my text anywhere?

No. The tool runs fully in your browser with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It only assembles the text you typed into a structured prompt. Nothing is uploaded or sent to any server by the tool itself.

What exactly do I get with the Pro Pack?

A downloadable Markdown file with 100 additional professional prompt templates, grouped by category. Each has a title, when to use it, and template text with [PLACEHOLDERS] you fill in. It is delivered digitally right after purchase.

How do I receive the Pro Pack after paying?

Payment is processed by Creem as Merchant of Record. After successful payment, Creem shows you the download for the Pro Pack file. You keep the file forever.

What if I want a refund?

The Pro Pack is covered by a 14-day refund policy from the purchase date. Email hessan.amer91@gmail.com with your order details. Full terms are in our Refund Policy.
